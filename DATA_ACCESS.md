# Data access

This repository does not redistribute participant-level data, controlled mortality records, licensed summary statistics or molecular source matrices. Researchers must obtain each resource from its original provider.

## Longitudinal cohorts

| Resource | Public access route | Repository status |
|---|---|---|
| China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study (CHARLS) | <https://charls.charlsdata.com/> | Not redistributed |
| English Longitudinal Study of Ageing (ELSA) | <https://www.elsa-project.ac.uk/accessing-elsa-data> | Not redistributed |
| Health and Retirement Study (HRS) | <https://hrs.isr.umich.edu/data-products> | Not redistributed |
| Mexican Health and Aging Study (MHAS) | <https://www.mhasweb.org/> | Not redistributed |
| Survey of Health, Ageing and Retirement in Europe (SHARE) | <https://share-eric.eu/data/data-access> | Not redistributed |

deCODE and Fenland cis-pQTL resources and the publication-derived single-cell, immune-age and spatial-proteomic resources are identified in the article and its Supplementary Information. Their source files are excluded from this repository under the applicable access and redistribution terms.

Provider websites are authoritative for current versions, acknowledgements and access conditions. This repository supplies analysis logic and does not confer rights to third-party data.
